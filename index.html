DreamLog Privacy Policy

Your dreams are yours. We take privacy seriously.

Last updated: March 11, 2026

Summary: DreamLog stores your dream journal entries locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data. AI processing is done via secure API calls. You are always in control of your data.

1. Information We Collect

DreamLog collects only the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Dream entries — titles, descriptions, moods, and tags you write. Stored locally on your device.
  • App usage data — anonymous analytics to improve performance (no personal identifiers).
  • Purchase information — processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not see your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI dream interpretation via our secure API
  • To generate AI dream artwork based on your dream content
  • To send morning reminder notifications (only if you enable this)
  • To process your premium subscription through Apple

3. AI Processing

When you request an AI interpretation or dream image, your dream content is sent securely to our AI processing service (fal.ai). This data is:

  • Transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Used only to generate your interpretation/image
  • Not stored permanently on our servers
  • Not used to train AI models
  • Not shared with third parties for marketing

4. Data Storage

Your dream journal is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage. We do not back up your dreams to our servers. If you delete the app, your local data will be deleted.

5. Third-Party Services

  • fal.ai — AI image and text generation. Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management. Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store — Payment processing. Privacy Policy →

6. Notifications

DreamLog may send you a morning reminder notification to record your dreams. This feature is optional and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Notifications or in your device's Settings app.

7. Children's Privacy

DreamLog is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

  • Access — All your data is visible to you within the app
  • Delete — Delete individual dreams or all data from Settings
  • Export — Use the share feature to export dream entries
  • Opt-out — Disable notifications at any time

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all API communications. Your dream data on your device is protected by iOS's built-in security features.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app. Continued use of DreamLog after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
